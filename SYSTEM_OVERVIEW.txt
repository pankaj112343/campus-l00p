┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                           TARGET PRIORITY FORMULA                            │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

  Priority Score = (Distance × 0.5) + (Angle × 2.0) + (Health/100 × 10)
                   + (HelmetLevel × 5) - (IsAttacking ? 50 : 0)
  
  Lower score = Higher priority
  
  Examples:
  • Attacking enemy at 20m, 10° angle, 50 HP, Level 1 helmet
    = (20×0.5) + (10×2.0) + (50/100×10) + (1×5) - 50 = -10 (HIGHEST)
  
  • Passive enemy at 50m, 30° angle, 100 HP, Level 3 helmet
    = (50×0.5) + (30×2.0) + (100/100×10) + (3×5) - 0 = 110 (LOWEST)
